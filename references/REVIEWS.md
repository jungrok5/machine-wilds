## 2026-11-28 · the wings and sky previews (WU WG WR WA WL WS) and the 2:11 X cut

### Cinematography
1. WR: the chase camera passes through Ridge's superstructure (3.2–4.6 s); the top-down camera after touchdown is 6.5 s
   of dark clutter. Keep 0–2.5 s, then a camera fixed in Ridge's frame (deck level at the bow looking aft, or outboard);
   cut 1.5 s after touchdown.
2. Nimbus never looks enormous: the walker and the 130 m hull fill the same angle. Put the camera near the hull, pitch
   ≤ 15°, light from behind or the side; end WL on a pull-back that shows the whole ship with the walker a dot.
3. WS: the hull never reads (350 m off, looking up 30° into the base). Side-on from 600–800 m, ≤ 15° up, fov 24–30, a
   foreground treeline and the walker; no flash before 2 s; true dark between flashes; end on the longest strike.
4. WU falls apart halfway (wing near the lens, a 150° whip, a birch hides the walker): split into two shots, cut on the
   wing-open frame.
5. WG is wallpaper: track in profile, spring the camera, horizon level, fly higher, give it a goal on the horizon.
6. Flight shots are all "behind the walker, centred": never cut two together.
7. Light continuity: WU/WG/WR belong at tod ~18.45–18.6; B at ~18.3.
8. X cut: DAWN starts after its light change; V2, DS, SNOW, PAN are dead; N1 misses the lit head; C ends on the walker
   backing into the lens; the film ends on its dimmest image. A giant looks huge from low and far (≥ 3× its height,
   fov 25–35, walker in the lower third) — H1 is the model; add a high wide VISTA.

### VFX and lighting
1. Lightning is a global brightness-up (frame median 50 → 165–229, interiors lit like outdoors): a light with a position
   and visibility, true dark between strokes; night storm p50 ≈ 25–35, flash peak ≈ 100–130.
2. Nimbus is buried (OD 3–8 between eye and gondola); its reveal shadow is two ellipsoids. Let the gondola hang below
   the base, a thin pocket round the hull, a shadow map from the flash, flashes walking bow → stern, its own lights.
3. The storm cloud is a lone cotton ball with a cut-out edge in a clear sky: grow the bank out of an overcast deck.
4. The cloud reads low-res and smooth: transmittance to inner lights, multiple-scattering octaves, finer noise,
   edge-aware upsampling.
5. Night and rain: milky blacks (p1 ≈ 38): lift, grain, adaptation and contrast retuned; night p1 ≈ 4–10.
6. Rain: long opaque streaks that radiate when looking up, white ring splashes, dry surfaces.
7. Lamps are hard discs with no glare or spill. 8. Steam and debris unlit. 9. No motion blur.
10. Weak aerial perspective on clear days. 11. The envelope at sunset is flat. 12. Bolts are one width, few branches.

### Animation
1. Landing has no animation and is hidden by a cut (legs teleport, pitch snaps, speed cut in a frame, no knee give).
2. The close approach stalls and hovers; the steering flips yaw frame to frame.
3. Take-off has no anticipation; lift comes before the wings.
4. Beats have no power: the body ignores the stroke. 5. The glide is frozen.
6. In flight the neck and legs hang: it reads upright under a plank.
7. Primaries fan 70° like a paper fan. 8. The arm's unfold is rubbery. 9. The close collapses out of order.
10. Banking yaws before it rolls. 11. No secondary motion (antenna, folded wings on the ground).
12. The hop gathers for 0.1 s. Giants: nothing broken seen.

### Art direction
1. Nimbus has no silhouette or scale (flat fins read as ears). 2. The envelope looks like a metal tank: pillow the
   panels, bigger patches, backlit canvas glow; its "07" reads mirrored and repeats Ridge's.
3. The gondola is a box on a scaffold: a hull below, a stacked town above.
4. The landing pad is a modern heliport: an oxide-red ring and amber lanterns.
5. The wings read as white paper fans: folded flat along the back, wider vanes, metal not ceramic; no cyan on the body.
6. The storm cloud is a cut-out. 7. Rods and engines too small to matter.
8. Far forest a speckled carpet, hill tiling visible, lime saplings pop. 9. Bare dirt mounds.
10. Blue "sticker" panels on Ridge's legs; rust reads as camouflage; feet are discs.
11. WR's roof domes are untextured spheres. 12. The cast has no shared livery. 13. Flat values in the sky shots.
